- **Open Precursor from your phone.** **Mobile access** in the persona menu starts a private Microsoft dev tunnel and shows a QR code: scan it, sign in with the tunnel's account, and your phone opens your own Precursor while everything keeps running on your computer. Each phone is paired with a one-time code and can be signed out from the same dialog; the tunnel keeps its address, comes back after a restart and restarts itself when it drops.
